Frequently asked questions

Why didn't a reminder arrive?

Enable reminders for the task and allow notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → Last Done. Recurring reminders need a first completion and a future reminder time. Focus modes, notification settings, and system scheduling can affect delivery.

Last Done schedules the nearest future reminders first, reserving up to 60 pending notifications across the app. Open the app periodically to refresh the queue. Missed reminder times are not automatically sent again.

How do I correct a completion?

Open the task's detail page and edit or delete the relevant history entry. The app recalculates the last completion and next due date. Immediately after marking a task complete, you can also use the brief Undo action.

What is the difference between archiving and deleting?

Archiving keeps completion history and pauses reminders; the task can be restored. Deleting a task permanently removes its history from the app and cancels its reminders.

Are my records synced or backed up?

Last Done stores records locally and does not offer account-based sync, app-managed cloud sync, or a separate backup service. System device backups may include app data depending on your settings. The developer cannot retrieve your local records.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Last Done is designed for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. English and Simplified Chinese are available in Settings, along with system, light, and dark appearance options.

常见问题

为什么没有收到提醒？

请开启事项提醒，并在 iOS 设置 → 通知 → Last Done 中允许通知。周期提醒需要先有一次完成记录，且提醒时间尚未过去。专注模式、通知设置和系统调度也可能影响送达。

应用优先安排最近的未来提醒，最多预留 60 条待发送通知。请定期打开应用刷新队列。已错过的提醒不会自动补发。

如何纠正完成记录？

打开事项详情，编辑或删除对应的历史记录，应用会重新计算上次完成时间和下次到期日期。刚标记完成时，也可以使用短暂显示的撤销操作。

归档和删除有什么区别？

归档保留完成历史、暂停提醒，可以恢复事项。删除会从应用中永久移除该事项及其历史，并取消提醒。

记录会同步或备份吗？

记录保存在设备本地。Last Done 不提供账号同步、应用级云同步或独立备份服务。根据设备设置，系统备份可能包含应用数据。开发者无法找回你的本地记录。

支持哪些设备和语言？

Last Done 面向 iOS 17 及以上的 iPhone。可以在设置中选择英文、简体中文，以及跟随系统、浅色或深色外观。
