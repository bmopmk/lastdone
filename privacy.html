Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Applies to the Last Done iOS app and this website.

Your tasks and completion history stay on your device.

1. Information in the app

Last Done stores the tasks you create, including their names, notes, categories, recurrence settings, reminder settings, and completion history, in its local app storage. App preferences such as language and appearance are also stored locally.

The app does not require an account and does not transmit this information to the developer. It has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics, tracking, or app-managed cloud sync. The developer does not sell your personal information.

2. Local reminders

If you enable reminders, Last Done asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications using Apple's on-device notification framework. Reminders are not delivered through a developer-operated push server. You can disable reminders for individual tasks or manage permission in iOS Settings.

Task titles may appear on the lock screen or in notification previews. You can control notification previews in iOS Settings.

3. Retention, deletion, and backups

Task information remains in the app until you delete it. Deleting a task removes its completion history and cancels its reminders. Archiving preserves the history and pauses reminders.

Depending on your device settings, Apple's device backups may include app data. Last Done does not offer its own cloud backup or sync service. Removing the app does not delete copies in existing system backups; manage those backups through Apple or your device settings.

4. Support email

If you email hredswbmopmk@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Avoid sending sensitive task content unless needed for support.

Support messages are processed through Gmail and are subject to Google's handling of that service. Messages are kept for as long as needed to address your request and related follow-up. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

5. This website and external services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, for security and service operation. See GitHub's privacy statement. This website does not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Opening this website or your email app from Last Done involves the corresponding browser, hosting, or email service. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this page if Last Done's data practices change and revise the date above. For privacy questions, contact the Last Done developer at hredswbmopmk@gmail.com.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日 · 适用于 Last Done iOS 应用及本网站。

事项与完成历史保存在你的设备上。

1. 应用中的信息

Last Done 将你创建的事项名称、备注、分类、周期设置、提醒设置及完成历史保存在应用的本地存储中。语言与外观等偏好设置也保存在本地。

应用不要求账号，不向开发者传输上述信息，不使用广告 SDK、第三方统计或跟踪，也不提供应用级云同步。开发者不会出售你的个人信息。

2. 本地提醒

启用提醒时，Last Done 会申请通知权限，并使用 Apple 设备端通知框架安排本地通知，不通过开发者运营的推送服务器发送提醒。你可以关闭单个事项的提醒，也可以在 iOS 设置中管理通知权限。

事项标题可能出现在锁屏或通知预览中。你可以在 iOS 设置中控制通知预览。

3. 保留、删除与备份

事项信息保留在应用中，直到你将其删除。删除事项会删除对应完成历史并取消提醒；归档会保留历史并暂停提醒。

根据设备设置，Apple 的设备备份可能包含应用数据。Last Done 没有自己的云备份或同步服务。卸载应用不会删除已有系统备份中的副本；请通过 Apple 或设备设置管理这些备份。

4. 支持邮件

向 hredswbmopmk@gmail.com 发邮件时，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址及你主动提供的信息和附件，用于回复请求和处理问题。除非支持问题确有需要，请避免发送敏感事项内容。

支持邮件通过 Gmail 处理，适用 Google 对该服务的数据处理规则。邮件会在处理请求及相关后续事项所需的期间保留。你可以联系我们申请删除支持邮件，但适用法律要求保留的情况除外。

5. 本网站及外部服务

本网站托管于 GitHub Pages。GitHub 可能为安全及服务运行处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息，详情参见 GitHub 隐私声明。本网站不添加统计、广告、Cookie 或跟踪脚本。

从 Last Done 打开网站或邮件应用时，会使用相应的浏览器、托管或邮件服务，这些服务适用其各自的隐私政策。

6. 政策更新与联系

如果 Last Done 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页及上方日期。隐私问题请联系 Last Done 开发者：hredswbmopmk@gmail.com。
